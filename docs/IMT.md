# What we know about IMT Ghaziabad

Background for building and maintaining IMT Never Sleeps. Sources are marked:
**handbook** (the student handbooks in `all files/`), **files** (the schedules,
allocation sheets and student lists in `all files/`), **Khush**, and **public**
(imt.edu and education sites, read on 6 Oct 2026). Where sources disagree, the
college's own files win over a handbook, and a handbook wins over public pages.
Handbooks say their dates and structures are tentative.

Last updated: 8 Oct 2026.

## Programmes and roll numbers

A roll number is batch + programme code + serial: `25 0103 045` is the batch
that joined in 2025, programme `0103`, student 045. (files)

| Code | Programme | Schedule group | 2025-27 | 2026-28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0101` | PGDM | core | 150 | 134 |
| `0102` | PGDM Finance | core | 31 | 26 |
| `0103` | PGDM Marketing | core | 178 | 178 |
| `0201` | PGDM DCP (Dual Country Program) | dcp | 239 | 96 |
| `0601` | PGDM BFS (Banking and Financial Services) | bfs | list not received (about 151 sat the Sep 2026 exams) | 146 |
| `FPM` | Doctoral students sitting PGDM courses | core | 2 | 2 in the exam plan |

Student counts are from the lists received. A "schedule group" is a set of
programmes that share one weekly schedule: PGDM, PGDM Marketing and PGDM
Finance are taught together as **core**; **BFS** and **DCP** each have their
own schedule, course list and sections.

Earlier guesses that were wrong, kept here so they are not repeated: `0601` is
BFS, not DCP; `0201` is DCP. The 2025-27 DCP batch has 239 students, far more
than the 96 of 2026-28.

There are also executive and part-time PGDMs. (public)

## Shape of the two years

- Every year has three terms. Terms 1-3 are year one, Terms 4-6 year two. (Khush)
- Year one is core courses taught **section-wise**: the 2026-28 core batch has
  six sections A-F in rooms 401-402 and 501-504; BFS and DCP have sections A
  and B. (files)
- Electives start in Term 4. From then the core batch's schedule is per course
  and per course-section, and each student has a Major and a Minor. (Khush, files)
- The course list changes every term and every year. (Khush)
- Promotion to year two needs a CGPA of at least 5.0 at the end of Term 3 and
  no more than "1 F and 1 D" or "3 Ds". (handbook)
- One credit is 10 classroom hours, so a 3-credit course is 30 hours, which is
  24 sessions of 75 minutes. That is the "24" a session counter runs to. (handbook)
- SSR is **Sustainability and Social Responsibility**, a 2-credit practice
  course; "SSR Visits" in a schedule are its field visits. (handbook)

## Credits and course lists

### PGDM (core), 2025-27 and 2026-28 (handbook)

- 2025-27 diploma: at least 107 credits = 36 core + 23 practice + 48 elective,
  plus the foundation courses. PGDM Marketing and PGDM Finance students must
  take their major from their own stream.
- **Foundation:** Microeconomics, Quantitative Methods for Business, Case
  Learning Pedagogy, Personal Growth Lab, Spread Sheet Modelling, Introduction
  to Accounting and Finance, Introduction to Management.
- **Core (3 credits each):** Marketing Management, Accounting for Business
  Decisions, Organizational Behavior, Macroeconomics for Managers, Operations
  Management, Business and Corporate Finance, Managerial Accounting, Human
  Resource Management, Digital Business Strategy, Entrepreneurial Manager,
  Strategic Management, Data Analytics and AI for Business; plus Indian
  Knowledge Systems for Contemporary Leaders (non-credit, 2026-28).
- **Practice:** Summer Internship Project, Business Research or Startup
  Incubation, Technology Readiness, Business Environment, Business
  Communication (3 each); Design Thinking, Critical Thinking, Sustainability
  and Social Responsibility, Legal Aspects of Business (2 each).
- **Marketing major (2026-28):** eight "core of major" courses (Consumer
  Behavior, Sales and Distribution Management, Integrated Marketing
  Communication, Digital Marketing, Marketing Research and Analytics, Product &
  Brand Management, Marketing of Services & Experience Management, Strategic
  Marketing with Markstrat; at least four required) and thirteen
  specialisation courses. The other areas' lists are on the following handbook
  pages (PGDM 2026-28 handbook, pages 28-31) and not yet transcribed.

### PGDM BFS 2026-28 (handbook)

- **Foundation:** the seven above plus Introduction to Python and Introduction
  to Data Visualization.
- **Core:** Financial Accounting, Introduction to Banking and Financial
  Services, Corporate Finance, Organizational Structure and Behaviour,
  Financial Statement Analysis, Risk Management in BFS-I and -II, Business
  Valuation, Introduction to Financial Markets, Marketing Management, Corporate
  Banking, Service Excellence in Banking Projects, Fintech, Investment Banking,
  Marketing of Financial Services, Financing Large Projects, Banking and
  Treasury Management, Financial Derivatives, Mergers, Acquisitions and
  Corporate Restructuring, Wealth Management, Security Analysis and Portfolio
  Management, Venture Capital & Private Equity, Fixed Income Securities, BFSI &
  Society, Strategic Management, Human Resource Management.
- **Practice:** Business Communication, Macroeconomics & Monetary Policy,
  Summer Internship Project, Navigating Bloomberg and Reuters, NISM Industry
  Project, NISM Certification 1 and 2.
- **Non-core:** Basics of Database and SQL, Machine Learning for BFSI,
  Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Digital Strategy.
- **Electives:** only three in the whole programme: Elective 1 and Elective 2
  in Term 5, and an open elective in Term 6. This matches what Khush said for
  the 2025-27 BFS batch: two chosen subjects in Term 5, everything else common.

### PGDM DCP 2026-28 (handbook)

- 105 credits = 36 core + 21 practice + 36 major electives + 12 electives
  outside the major. Majors: Marketing, Operations and Supply Chain, Finance;
  the minor must be a different area.
- Core and practice lists match PGDM's, with Global Business Environment and
  Business Research Project in place of Business Environment and Business
  Research.
- Terms 3 and 4 carry major electives 1-10, Term 5 major 11 and minors 1-2,
  Term 6 major 12 and minors 3-4. Elective lists by name: 12 Marketing, 13
  Operations and Supply Chain, 12 Finance (handbook pages 33-34).

## How the week runs (files, week of 5 Oct 2026)

- Sessions are 75 minutes. Each group has its own slot times: 2025-27 core
  8:45 am - 8:00 pm in seven slots; 2026-28 core 9:15 am - 6:45 pm in six;
  BFS and DCP 9:00 am - 8:15 pm in up to seven.
- Classes run seven days a week, Saturday and Sunday included.
- A course usually meets in double blocks, two to four times a week.
- Whole-day or part-day events appear in the grid: "SSR Visits", "ADP
  Placement Process".

## Exams (files, Sep 2026 cycle)

Nine days, two sittings a day (10:00 am and 2:30 pm), 21 halls. First-years
mostly sat 6 to 8 papers, second-years 5 or 6.

## General (public)

IMT Ghaziabad is AACSB accredited. Its Dubai campus opened in 2006 in Dubai
International Academic City.
